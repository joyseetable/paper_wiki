## Questions from Ingest

- **Q**: How dependent is attention-guided token selection ([[GRAB]]) on backbone quality? If the underlying CLIP model has noisy/uninformative attention maps, does the method degrade gracefully or collapse? Sparked by [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]
- **Q**: Does GRAB generalize beyond person search to other fine-grained VL tasks (product retrieval, medical imaging, species classification)? The attention saliency phenomenon may be domain-dependent. Sparked by [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]
- **Q**: How sensitive are MARS hyperparameters (discard ratio δ=0.25, Middle+Late layer selection) to domain shift? Are these dataset-specific or universal? Sparked by [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]
- **Q**: The paper claims "no extra supervision" but still uses CLIP — which was pretrained on 400M image-text pairs. Where do we draw the line on what counts as "external supervision"? Sparked by [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]
- **Q**: Can the Attentive Bank idea be applied to pure text tasks? e.g., selecting high-saliency tokens from self-attention for long-context summarization? Sparked by [[GRAB]]

## Questions from TARA Ingest

- **Q**: TARA is only tested in biological domains — do other domains (product hierarchies, medical taxonomies, document categories) have pretrained "foundation models" that could serve as equivalent teachers? Sparked by [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]
- **Q**: How to determine the optimal alignment layers (ℓ for visual, m for label) without exhaustive grid search? The paper tried 14/28 manually — is there a signal (e.g., representation similarity metrics) that predicts which layers will benefit most? Sparked by [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]
- **Q**: What is the minimum viable BFM teacher? If you trained a BFM on a much smaller dataset (e.g., 10K species instead of 450K), would TARA still see gains, or is the BFM's scale/structure the critical factor? Sparked by [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]
- **Q**: Connection to [[GRAB]] — both papers use a "teacher" representation to guide LMM/CLIP intermediate features. Is "representation alignment with a structured teacher" a general recipe that works across tasks? Sparked by cross-reading [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]] and [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]
- **Q**: TARA uses No-Thinking RFT which actively suppresses chain-of-thought — but on ImageWikiQA (complex VQA), TARA still helps. Does this mean better visual representations compensate for suppressed reasoning, or does No-Thinking RFT actually produce better internal reasoning that's just not verbalized? Sparked by [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]

## Questions from Fine-R1 Ingest

- **Q**: TARA (No-Thinking RFT + BFM alignment) vs. Fine-R1 (CoT SFT + TAPO) — same lab, same base models, same task (FGVR/HVR). When should we use knowledge injection (TARA) vs. knowledge deployment (Fine-R1)? Are they complementary — can you do both? Sparked by cross-reading [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]] and [[Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]
- **Q**: Fine-R1's CoT data is generated by Qwen2.5-VL-32B, a much stronger model. What's the dependency on teacher quality? If you used a weaker teacher (e.g., same-scale model), would the CoT SFT still work? Sparked by [[Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]
- **Q**: TAPO requires ground-truth labels for triplet selection (positive/negative neighbors). Can this be extended to truly open-world settings where you don't have labeled negatives? Maybe using embedding similarity as a proxy? Sparked by [[Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]
- **Q**: The knowledge-deployment hypothesis (H3) is demonstrated via PCA visualization but not causally tested. How would you design an intervention experiment to prove that Fine-R1 improves knowledge *deployment* specifically (not knowledge or features)? Sparked by [[Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]
- **Q**: Both Fine-R1 and [[ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]] make the same architectural choice — take a strong pretrained model (CLIP / Qwen-VL) and add a lightweight training mechanism. Is there a general principle here about "don't retrain, redirect" for adapting foundation models to specialized tasks?

## Questions from DIVA Ingest

- **Q**: DIVA only tests on 1.5B–8B models. Does the shared/unique factorization scale to larger models (20B+), or does the middle-layer divergence pattern change with scale? Sparked by [[DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]
- **Q**: The middle-layer range (8–18) is chosen empirically via diagnostic analysis. Is there a principled method to determine which layers to target — e.g., using gradient conflict metrics or effective rank as an automated selector? Sparked by [[DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]
- **Q**: DIVA is only tested on image modalities. Would the shared/unique factorization generalize to other modality pairs — video understanding+generation, audio+text, interleaved any-to-any? Sparked by [[DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]
- **Q**: How does DIVA's internal MI-based alignment compare to TARA's external BFM teacher alignment? Both align representations, but one uses cross-branch mutual information, the other uses a pretrained teacher. Could they be combined — external teacher for initial alignment + internal MI for refinement? Sparked by cross-reading [[DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]] and [[TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]
- **Q**: DIVA claims gains come from the method, not the data (Base+SFT shows negligible improvement). But the training data construction (captioning + inpainting from same sample) is itself a design choice. How much of DIVA's success is the factorization objective vs. the paired-flow data construction? Sparked by [[DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]

## Questions from IQA-Spider Ingest

- **Q**: Text-to-point grounding uses a coarse 2×2 spatial grid (left/right × top/bottom). Can this be extended to finer spatial resolution (e.g., 3×3 with center, or N×N grid) for multi-object grounding, or is there a fundamental precision limit from softmax dispersion over positional terms? Sparked by [[IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]
- **Q**: The grounding stage is training-free but depends on Stage 1 instruction tuning to teach the LMM to produce positional terms. How sensitive is grounding performance to the quality/quantity of Stage 1 tuning? If the base LMM already uses spatial language well, could you skip Stage 1 entirely? Sparked by [[IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]
- **Q**: The four-task IQA paradigm (global description, local description, grounding, referring) is defined for quality assessment — but could this multi-granularity task taxonomy generalize to other visual understanding domains? e.g., medical imaging (lesion description + grounding), remote sensing, or FGVR (attribute description + part localization)? Sparked by [[IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]
- **Q**: Text-to-point grounding converts logits to a single (x,y) point. How would it handle cases requiring multiple point prompts (e.g., multiple disconnected distortion regions)? Would iterative prompting (segment → remove → re-prompt) work, or does the paradigm inherently assume single-target grounding? Sparked by [[IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]
- **Q**: IQA-Spider only tests on 4B–7B models. Larger LMMs may have richer spatial language and better positional term logit distributions — could text-to-point grounding benefit from scale in ways that special-token grounding does not? Sparked by [[IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]

## Questions from SG-SRL Ingest

- **Q**: SG-SRL decouples semantic learning from form learning via recovery SFT. Could this decoupling work for other RL settings where reward hacking is structural — e.g., length bias in summarization, verbosity in instruction following? Sparked by [[SG-SRL-crosslingual-semantic-rl]]
- **Q**: The reranker reward + safeguard approach achieves semantic gains despite reward hacking. Is the "accept the hacked intermediate, recover later" pattern generalizable to other RLHF/deployment pipelines? Sparked by [[SG-SRL-crosslingual-semantic-rl]]

## Questions from AGSM Ingest

- **Q**: AGSM trains only soft tokens (1.8M params) while keeping the diffusion backbone frozen. This is a strong instance of "don't retrain, redirect" — could soft-token-only training work for FGVR LMMs? Training a few learnable prefix tokens instead of full TARA alignment might be far cheaper. Sparked by [[AGSM-alignment-guided-score-matching]]
- **Q**: AGSM's dual-token design (ψ+ for positive guidance, ψ− for negative guidance, drop ψ− at inference) explicitly separates alignment signal from contrastive signal. Could TARA adopt this — train a separate "negative BFM token" to push away from wrong taxonomic branches? Sparked by [[AGSM-alignment-guided-score-matching]]

## Questions from CGPO Ingest

- **Q**: CGPO shows that training-time critic guidance + unguided deployment avoids inference overhead. TARA also follows this pattern (teacher at training, discard at inference). Is there a general principle: "expensive guidance during training, cheap inference"? Sparked by [[CGPO-critic-guided-diffusion-policy]]
- **Q**: CGPO's DSG constraint keeps guided steps on the diffusion manifold. Is there an analogous constraint for representation alignment — e.g., ensuring BFM-aligned features stay within the LMM's representational manifold to avoid degradation? Sparked by [[CGPO-critic-guided-diffusion-policy]]
