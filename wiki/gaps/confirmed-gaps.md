# Confirmed Gaps

Problems that 3+ papers acknowledge as unsolved. These are the most reliable starting points for new ideas.

## How to Use This File
- Each gap is a `##` section with evidence from papers
- Link to the papers that confirm the gap exists
- When a gap is solved, move it to the bottom under "Resolved"

## G1: Cross-domain generalization of fine-grained methods is completely unverified

**Problem**: Every method in the wiki claims to solve a general problem (fine-grained alignment, knowledge deployment, multi-granularity understanding) but tests on a single domain. No method has been validated across domains.

**Evidence**:
- [[papers/TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]: Only tested in biological domains (iNat-Plant, iNat-Animal, TerraIncognita). "Hierarchical taxonomies exist in many domains (products, documents, medical)" — but none tested.
- [[papers/ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]: Only tested on person search (TBPS). "Does attention-guided token selection generalize to other fine-grained VL tasks (product retrieval, medical imaging)?"
- [[papers/DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]: Only tested on image understanding+generation. Video, audio, interleaved modalities unexplored.
- [[papers/IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]: Only tested on IQA. Multi-granularity task taxonomy could generalize to medical imaging, remote sensing, FGVR — untested.

**Why it matters**: If methods don't generalize, the field is overfitting to benchmark-specific patterns rather than discovering general principles. The "don't retrain, redirect" pattern may be an artifact of domain-specific pretraining, not a general recipe.

## G2: Scalability of methods to larger models (7B+) is unknown

**Problem**: Most methods are tested on small-to-medium models (1.5B–8B). Whether the gains hold, diminish, or amplify at larger scales (20B+) is unknown.

**Evidence**:
- [[papers/DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]: "Only tested on 1.5B–8B models — scalability to larger models unverified"
- [[papers/IQA-Spider-multi-granularity-quality]]: "Only tested on 4B–7B models; scaling behavior unknown"
- [[papers/Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]: Fine-R1-3B beats 7B general MLLMs, but 7B→32B extrapolation unknown. CoT teacher is 32B but student scaling is uncharacterized.

**Why it matters**: If gains diminish with scale, these methods are transitional — useful now but irrelevant in the 32B+ era. If gains amplify, they're foundational infrastructure.

## G3: Teacher/guidance signal quality dependency is unexamined across all methods

**Problem**: Every method uses an external guidance signal (BFM teacher, CoT teacher model, CLIP attention), but none systematically ablates how guidance quality affects final performance. The "minimum viable teacher" is unknown for any method.

**Evidence**:
- [[papers/TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]: "What is the minimum viable BFM teacher? Can a weaker teacher still help?" BioCLIP2 is a strong teacher trained on 450K species — dependency on its scale/structure is unknown.
- [[papers/Fine-R1-fine-grained-recognition]]: CoT SFT data is generated by Qwen2.5-VL-32B, a much stronger model. "What's the dependency on teacher quality? If you used a weaker teacher (same-scale model), would CoT SFT still work?"
- [[papers/ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]: "The method relies on CLIP's attention quality — untested on weaker backbones. What happens when attention maps are noisy or uninformative?"

**Why it matters**: If methods are robust to weak teachers, they're widely deployable. If they require strong teachers, the real contribution is the teacher, not the method — and domains without strong teachers (medical, legal, niche scientific) are left out.

## G4: Critical hyperparameters (intervention layers, token budgets) are chosen empirically without principled diagnostics

**Problem**: All methods that intervene on specific layers or tokens select them via empirical search or heuristic. No principled diagnostic exists to predict optimal intervention points a priori.

**Evidence**:
- [[papers/TARA-taxonomy-aware-alignment]]: Optimal alignment layers (ℓ=14 for visual, m=28 for label) found by exhaustive grid search. "How to determine these without exhaustive search?"
- [[papers/DIVA-representation-divergence-mutual-reinforcement]]: Middle-layer range (8–18) empirically chosen via diagnostic analysis. "Is there a principled method to determine which layers to target — e.g., using gradient conflict metrics?"
- [[papers/ITSELF-attention-guided-alignment]]: MARS discard ratio δ=0.25 and layer selection (Middle+Late) are dataset-tuned. "How sensitive are these to domain shift?"

**Why it matters**: Empirical layer selection makes each new application of these methods require expensive hyperparameter search. A principled diagnostic would turn a 2D search into a 1D measurement.
